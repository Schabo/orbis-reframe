# DJI 360 cameras file format — `.osv`

**Desk research (2026-10-15), no DJI file examined.** From public specs and
Gyroflow's telemetry-parser (`src/dji/`, MIT OR Apache-2.0), which ships the
protobuf schemas the cameras write. Markers as in [`README.md`](README.md);
the rules there on what may be taken from others' reverse engineering apply
here in particular: telemetry-parser's DJI stitching code partly cites DJI
Studio's internals.

Covers the DJI Osmo 360 (2025), Osmo 360 II, Osmo 360 Air and the Avata 360
drone.

## 1. Files and streams

- 📖 360 video is recorded as `.OSV`; single-lens and flat modes as `.MP4`.
  Final Cut Pro doesn't open `.OSV` directly (DJI Studio exports MP4).
- 📖 Osmo 360: two square ~1" sensors, f/1.9. 360 video up to 8K 50
  (8K 30 for long takes), 6K, and 4K 100. **10-bit is available in every
  mode**, with D-Log M as a log profile. ❓ Codec, presumably H.265;
  ❓ whether 8-bit stays the default.
- 📖 The container is MP4 / QuickTime with **two video tracks, one per
  lens** -- the X5's shape. Which track holds the **master** lens (the one
  at yaw ≈ 0) differs by product: track 1 on the Osmo 360, track 0 on the
  Osmo 360 II, Osmo 360 Air and Avata 360 (telemetry-parser; it's a
  format fact, but unverified on our files).
- ❓ Per-track picture size per mode, frame-rate details, audio streams,
  proxy files.

## 2. Metadata track

- 📖 A timed metadata track (`djmd`, with a `DJI meta` / `CAM meta`
  marker) carries one protobuf packet per video frame.
- 📖 The schema differs by product and is **chosen by product name**, not by
  the `proto_file_name` the clip declares (DJI reuses one schema under
  different declared names):

  | product | schema | `pano_dewarp_params` at `StreamMeta` field |
  |---|---|---|
  | Osmo 360 | `dvtm_oq101.proto` | 6 |
  | Osmo 360 II, Osmo 360 Air, Avata 360 | `dvtm_eagle4_wa530.proto` | 5 |

  Parsing with the wrong one silently drops the calibration (the wire types
  disagree). The product name is in `ClipMeta.clip_meta_header`.
- 📖 Packet layout (`ProductMeta`, Osmo 360): `clip_meta` (header, distortion,
  sensor readout time and read direction, IMU sampling rate, sensor frame
  rate, line readout `LRO_value`, sensor resolution, f-number…),
  `stream_meta` (video stream, colour mode, FOV type, the lens calibration,
  the lens mode `extri_lens_mode`), `frame_meta` (per frame: camera --
  exposure, ISO, white balance, attitude --; IMU; gimbal).

## 3. Lens calibration

- 📖 `PanoDewarpParams` holds **24 calibrations**, a slave/master pair for
  each of 12 cases: native, native-refined, refined at infinity ("far"),
  refined at stated distances (`far_07` … `far_16`), lens guards, and the
  waterproof case above and under water.
- 📖 Each `DewarpParams`: `fx fy cx cy`, `width height`, `yaw pitch roll`,
  `k1…k9`, `xi`, tangential `p` / `tangent_coeff`, `lens_model`, an occlusion
  polygon (`occlusion_pt_x/y`: the body in the picture), temperature
  compensation, `cam_extri_q` (pose between the two camera modules) and
  `cam_imu_extri_q` (**camera-to-IMU rotation**).
- 📖 The lens model telemetry-parser reads is an **equidistant θ-polynomial**
  with a tangential pair:

  ```text
  θ_d = θ·(1 + k1θ² + k2θ⁴ + k3θ⁶ + k4θ⁸ + k5θ¹⁰)
  x' = θ_d·X/ρ,  y' = θ_d·Y/ρ
  xd = x' + 2p1·x'y' + p2(θ_d² + 2x'²),   yd = y' + p1(θ_d² + 2y'²) + 2p2·x'y'
  u  = fx·xd + cx,  v = fy·yd + cy
  ```

  `xi` reads 0 on this path; it belongs to the unified-sphere (MEI) model the
  same `lens_model` field can select. The app's stitch implements MEI only
  (X5 §5.3), so this needs a second model.
- 📖 `yaw pitch roll` and `cam_extri_q` describe the same pose (the
  quaternion is the triple composed `Ry(yaw)·Rz(roll)·Rx(pitch)`), camera
  from rig, in a frame whose up is `z` (pitch ≈ 90° is a level lens).
- 📖 No lens translation is stated: the stitching distance is baked into the
  "far" ladder of focal lengths instead.
- **Ours to measure** (see [`README.md`](README.md)): which of the 24 slots to
  stitch with by default and per accessory, the blend and seam widths, and
  how far off-axis each lens's picture is usable. telemetry-parser's values
  for these come from DJI Studio and aren't used.

## 4. IMU

- 📖 Raw gyro (rad/s) and accelerometer (g), stated at 1000 Hz, plus DJI's
  own fused attitude quaternion per reading, packed per video frame.
- 📖 Timing isn't exact from the stamps: the Osmo 360 II delivers a reading
  every ~995 µs, packets carry a fixed-offset stamp while the reading count
  drifts, and the IMU clock wanders against the frame clock (5 ms over
  5 minutes on an Osmo 360). telemetry-parser fits a straight line of
  anchor against reading index over neighbouring packets.
- 📖 Raw axes: `zyx` relative to the fused attitude in a 360 recording
  (Gyroflow's orientation notation, X5 §6.3).
- 📖 Rolling shutter: from `LRO_value` (ns per sensor line) × sensor height,
  18.3 ms on the Osmo 360; the generic `sensor_readout_time` (24.0 ms) is not
  what DJI's 360 stabilization uses.
